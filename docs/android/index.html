How TellWell voice typing works

TellWell is a voice keyboard that sits next to the keyboard you already use. Set it up once, then switch to it whenever talking is easier than typing.

  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time.
  2. Download the models in TellWell: about 1 GB, once. Use Wi-Fi; it keeps going if you lock the phone.
  3. Allow the microphone and turn on the keyboard. Gboard stays your main keyboard.
  4. In any app, tap the globe key (or the keyboard icon at the bottom of the screen) and pick TellWell. It starts listening at once.
  5. Talk, then tap. TellWell types what you meant and switches back to your keyboard. Hold instead of tapping for push-to-talk.

Use it alongside Gboard voice typing

You don't have to give anything up. Keep typing on Gboard, keep its voice typing if you like it, and switch to TellWell when you want clean text rather than every word you said: TellWell removes filler words and repeated words, keeps only the final version when you correct yourself ("Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday"), and adds punctuation. It needs no account, and nothing you say leaves the phone.

A private speech to text app

  • On the phone. Speech recognition (NVIDIA Parakeet) and cleanup (S1-mini) run on your phone. What you say is never uploaded.
  • Microphone only while listening. It is on only while TellWell is open and listening, at most two minutes at a time. Audio stays in memory and is thrown away.
  • Nothing saved. No history, no logs, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account.
  • Never in password fields. TellWell refuses to listen there.
  • Internet only for the models. They are downloaded once from pinned Hugging Face versions and checked by SHA-256.
  • Open source. Read every line on GitHub.

Which Android phones it works on

Android 10 or newer on a 64-bit phone, which covers nearly every phone from the last five years, with about 1.1 GB of free memory while TellWell is open. TellWell checks each part of the phone with a known test sentence and uses the fastest one that gets it right: the NPU on recent Snapdragon chips, otherwise the Snapdragon GPU, otherwise the CPU, which works everywhere but is slower. On a Motorola Edge 70 (Snapdragon 7 Gen 4), text appears about one second after you tap. It has been tested on that phone so far; reports from other phones are welcome in the issues.

Also on Mac

TellWell for Mac does the same on Apple Silicon Macs: hold a key, speak, and clean text appears in any app, spelled the way names appear on your screen.

Questions

Is TellWell for Android free?

Yes. TellWell is open source under the MIT licence. There is no paid plan, no word limit, no account and no ads.

Does TellWell voice typing work offline?

Yes. After a one-time download of its models (about 1 GB), speech to text and cleanup run on your phone, so voice typing works without an internet connection. What you say is never sent anywhere.

Do I have to stop using Gboard?

No. Gboard, or whichever keyboard you use, stays your main keyboard. You switch to TellWell with the globe key when you'd rather talk, and TellWell switches back after typing your text.

Which Android phones does TellWell work on?

Android 10 or newer on a 64-bit phone, with about 1.1 GB of free memory while TellWell is open. It uses the NPU on recent Snapdragon chips, otherwise the Snapdragon GPU, otherwise the CPU. So far it has been tested on a Motorola Edge 70, where text appears about one second after you tap.

Is TellWell a free Wispr Flow alternative for Android?

For English, yes. Like Wispr Flow, TellWell turns speech into clean text in any app. TellWell is free with no word limit and runs on your phone. Wispr Flow has a free plan with limited words per week and a Pro plan from $12 per user per month billed annually (wisprflow.ai/pricing, 14 November 2026), and supports more languages.

Why does TellWell ask for internet access?

Only to download its two models once, from pinned versions on Hugging Face, checked by SHA-256. All of TellWell's network code is in one file you can read on GitHub. Nothing is ever uploaded.

Is TellWell on the Google Play Store?

Not yet. Download the APK from GitHub and open it on your phone; Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

Which languages does TellWell support?

TellWell is tuned for English.
